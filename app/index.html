Roadmap to the platinum ≈ 200–300 hours

Five stages, in order. Finish the story first, then the stranger missions, then the activities, then the collectibles — and clean up the 100% checklist last.

  1. Stage 1 — Story first

    Chapters 1–4 and the final mission. The 12 story trophies unlock automatically as you play; nothing here can be missed. Before you leave Chapter 1, read Positive Mental Attitude — the one trophy that might be missable — and keep a manual save.

  2. Stage 2 — Strangers of Leonida

    The stranger mission strands: Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret and the rest — eight strands in all, each ending in its own trophy, plus the silver for finishing all eight.

  3. Stage 3 — Activities

    Races, the shooting range (42 gold medals), 250 stunts, Top Fun drone races at all 14 locations, fishing, the arcade, meditation spots and bowling. The longest grind lives here.

  4. Stage 4 — Collectibles & the world

    100 vehicles discovered, 100 WAiNK locations, 3 card sets, every corner of Leonida explored, bank vaults, armored trucks and the money goals — $250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent, every garage bought.

  5. Stage 5 — Cleanup & platinum

    The 100% Completion checklist (35 tasks, under Progress after the story) and the gold for finishing it — then the platinum, Vice Got a Price!, pops on its own.
